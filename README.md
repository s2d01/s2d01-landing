## Google tag consent

`consent.js` and `consent.css` provide English/Italian opt-in controls on every page that previously loaded a Google tag, including legacy landing copies.

- No Google tag is loaded before a choice, or after rejecting both optional categories.
- Analytics (`G-8R69M3DQYM`) and Ads measurement (`AW-11465969104`) can be accepted separately.
- Advertising personalization and Google signals remain disabled.
- Spotify conversion events are sent only when Ads measurement is accepted; links work without consent.
- Choices expire after 180 days and can be changed through Cookie preferences / Preferenze cookie.
- Revocation disables the tags, removes relevant visible first-party Google cookies, and reloads the page.
- Page query strings/fragments are excluded and referrers are reduced to their origin.
- These controls cover Google tags, not independent behavior of pre-existing embedded third-party media.
- MOODS uses its own separate property and consent preference; changes here do not opt visitors into MOODS analytics.
