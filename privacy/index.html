Contact requests

The form collects your name, email, area of interest and message; company, phone, website or social profile, and sector are optional. I use these details to reply and handle your request, based on steps you request before a potential contract or my legitimate interest in managing enquiries.

The form is handled by Formspree, which receives and stores submissions in the S2D01 account. Submission archiving is enabled: on the Free plan, submissions older than 30 days are not deleted, but cannot be viewed unless the account upgrades. Submissions can be deleted manually from the archive. Formspree privacy information.

Cookies and external content

Your choice is stored in your browser for 180 days. Only with your consent, Google Analytics 4 measures visits and Google Ads measures conversions on Spotify links. Both services are provided by Google; form contents are not sent to Google. Ad personalisation and Google Signals remain disabled. You can change or withdraw your choice from Cookie preferences in the footer. Embedded content, such as Mixcloud, may send technical data to its provider when loaded.
