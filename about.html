About S2D01

S2D01 is an independent project dedicated to sound and creative technology, founded by Simone Cappelli.

Sound research, sound design, digital tools and AI solutions.

A non-linear path.

More than forty years across music, theatre, live performance, multimedia and digital technologies.

Artistic and technical skills: sound design, systems and networks, Apple/macOS environments, web development and digital solutions for creative and business activities. In recent years, the focus has been on artificial intelligence and automation to develop applications, organise information and simplify complex workflows.

An approach based on experimentation, operational independence and practical solutions, making technology useful in everyday work.

Collaborations
Available for flexible collaborations, including remote work, with creative agencies, businesses and professionals: digital tools, practical AI applications, multimedia solutions, and the review of websites and digital workflows before they reach the end user.

60's

1966: the first electric guitar, at just six years old. Played left-handed with the chords reversed, and already with a fair degree of command.

Signals

  • Electric guitar at age six
  • Left-handed, reversed chords
  • Curiosity about radios and amplifiers

70's

Deeper study of the acoustic guitar. Work as a hi-fi retailer, with first experiments in microphones, tape recorders, amplifiers, turntables, speakers and professional headphones. A lab for recording jingles and tape mashups. First radio shows, TV appearances and DJ sets. Wholesale sales of recorded music.

Signals

  • Music theory and solfège
  • Hands-on instrumental practice
  • Technical and perceptual skills for faithful sound reproduction
  • A brief but intense journey through the fleeting world of hosts and DJs
  • Sales and technical skills, with a solid knowledge of the music market

80's

Back to making music with other musicians, with a focus on synthesizers, drum machines and effects. In 1984, a stay in Paris helped build fluency in spoken French. In 1986, a diploma in theatre sound engineering, followed by the first collaborations with second-wave avant-garde theatre companies.

Signals

  • Training as a theatre sound engineer
  • Synthesis and electronic sound processing
  • Spoken French
  • Entry into second-wave avant-garde theatre

90's

1990 to 1994: an intense period of theatre, multimedia research and physical theatre, mainly with Krypton and Yves Lebreton’s Compagnia dell’Albero. A study of DOS on PC, and a first recording studio built around an Atari computer, Cubase, synthesizer racks, sequencers, an M1 master keyboard, a mixer and a Fostex 8-track recorder.

From 1994, HTML programming, graphic design with the Adobe suite and web design. Lo Stato dell’@rte, the first Italian web portal dedicated to music and performance, and websites including Farsetti Arte in Prato.

1997 to 2000, Laser Entertainment in Milan: the company LAN and website, servers and backup systems, work as a laser, audio and lighting technician, and music and jingles for events. Collaboration with the Teatro Grande in Brescia on the production and staging of operas for the Lombardy Opera Festival.

Signals

  • Krypton
  • Yves Lebreton
  • DOS
  • First recording studio
  • HTML programming, graphic and web design, IT infrastructure
  • Teatro Studio (Scandicci), Teatro Niccolini (Florence), Teatro Grande (Brescia), Teatro Bellini (Cremona)
  • Laser Entertainment (Milan)

00's

Moving to Follonica in 2000. Building one of Italy’s first online platforms for CIS Srl, along with many websites for hospitality companies. Learning Perl, Python and PHP, and working with MySQL databases in mixed Unix and macOS environments. Setting up a private server dedicated to digital audio.

Signals

  • Websites and online platforms
  • Graphic design and digital systems
  • Professional audio
  • Server infrastructure design and management

10's

2008 to 2015, between the Philippines and Italy: making music with Logic and building a website for a resort. In 2016, a second recording studio and the founding of S2D01.

Signals

  • The Philippines and Italy, 2008–2015
  • Second digital audio studio
  • S2D01 founded in 2016
  • Back to music and sound design

20's

Since 2020, S2D01 has been the centre of an independent practice where sound research, sound design, sonic memory and technology meet. Years of audio and multimedia experience are now the foundation for new tools and new ways of working.

Signals

  • MOODS: generative software for macOS
  • COS/KOS: AI, documentation and workflow automation
  • DAW MIDI Generator MCP: open-source project
  • AI, local models, MCP and automation
  • Web development with Astro, HTML, CSS and JavaScript
  • Quality control, testing and technical diagnosis
  • Sound design, generative music and MIDI generation
