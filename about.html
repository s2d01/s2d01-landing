About S2D01

S2D01 is an independent project dedicated to sound and creative technology, founded by Simone Cappelli.

Sound research, sound design, digital tools and AI solutions.

A non-linear path.

More than forty years across music, theatre, live performance, multimedia and digital technologies.

Artistic and technical skills: sound design, systems and networks, Apple/macOS environments, web development and digital solutions for creative and business activities. In recent years, the focus has been on artificial intelligence and automation to develop applications, organise information and simplify complex workflows.

An approach based on experimentation, operational independence and practical solutions, making technology useful in everyday work.

Collaborations
Available for flexible collaborations, including remote work, with creative agencies, businesses and professionals: digital tools, practical AI applications, multimedia solutions, and the review of websites and digital workflows before they reach the end user.

60's

In 1966, at the age of six, he received his first electric guitar. He played left-handed, with the chords reversed, and already had a fair command of the instrument.

Signals

  • Electric guitar at age six
  • Left-handed, reversed chords
  • Curiosity about radios and amplifiers

70's

An in-depth study of acoustic guitar, followed by hi-fi retail as progressive music spread. At last there was a concrete opportunity to experiment with microphones, tape recorders, amplifiers, turntables, speakers and professional headphones. A continuous laboratory of jingle recordings and tape mashups led to the first radio and television broadcasts and DJ sets. The decade ended with wholesale sales of recorded music products.

Signals

  • Music theory and solfège
  • Instrumental practice
  • Technical, perceptual and practical skills for accurate sound reproduction
  • A brief but intense journey through the fleeting world of presenters and DJs
  • Commercial and technical skills, with knowledge of the music market

80's

A return to playing music with other musicians, with a focus on synthesizers, electronic drums and effects. In 1984, in Paris, he deepened his knowledge of French. In 1986, he earned a diploma in theatre sound engineering. The first collaborations with second-avant-garde theatre companies followed.

Signals

  • Training as a theatre sound engineer
  • Synthesis and electronic processing
  • Spoken French
  • Entry into second-avant-garde theatre

90's

From 1990 to 1994, an intense period of theatre, multimedia research and physical theatre, especially with Krypton and Yves Lebreton’s Compagnia dell’Albero. He studied DOS on PC and built his first recording studio with an Atari computer, Cubase, synthesizer racks, an M1 master keyboard, mixer and Fostex 8-track recorder. From 1994, he developed skills in HTML programming, graphic design with Adobe Suite and web design. He created Lo Stato dell'@rte, the first Italian web portal dedicated to music and performance, as well as websites including Farsetti Arte in Prato. From 1997 to 2000, at Laser Entertainment in Milan, he worked on the LAN, the company website, servers and backup systems, while also serving as a laser, audio and lighting technician and producing music and jingles for events. He collaborated with Teatro Grande in Brescia on the production and staging of operas as part of the Lombardy Opera Festival.

Signals

  • Krypton
  • Yves Lebreton
  • DOS
  • First recording studio
  • HTML programming, graphic and web design, IT infrastructure
  • Teatro Studio (Scandicci), Teatro Niccolini (Florence), Teatro Grande (Brescia), Teatro Bellini (Cremona)
  • Laser Entertainment (Milan)

00's

In 2000, he moved to Follonica. He created one of Italy’s first online platforms for CIS Srl, along with many other websites for hospitality companies. He acquired knowledge of Perl and Python, and of MySQL and PHP databases, working across Unix and macOS environments. He also installed a private server dedicated to digital audio.

Signals

  • Websites and online platforms
  • Graphic design and digital systems
  • Professional audio
  • Server infrastructure design and management

10's

A second recording studio and the foundation of S2D01.

Signals

  • Digital audio studio #2
  • S2D01 founded in 2016
  • Return to composition and sound design

20's

Since 2020 S2D01 has been the centre of an independent practice where composition, sound design, sonic memory and technology meet. Audio and multimedia experience now supports new tools and new ways of working.

Signals

  • MOODS: generative software for macOS
  • COS/KOS: AI, documentation and workflow automation
  • DAW MIDI Generator MCP: open-source project
  • Sonic research and creative technology
