About S2D01

S2D01 is the music project of Simone Cappelli.

Based in Florence, Simone works across electronic music, ambient downtempo, sound design and cinematic atmospheres.

Simone Cappelli

Simone Cappelli is an Italian musician, sound designer and audio technologist.

His path moves through analog instruments, radio, electronic composition, digital audio, spatial sound and generative systems.

Under the name S2D01, he develops a personal language between electronic music, ambient downtempo, sound design and cinematic atmospheres.

His work starts from carefully built and selected sound materials: textures, sequences, piano, bass, drum machines, field recording and audio treatments.

S2D01 is not tied to a single genre. It is a personal space for musical research, where technology and listening remain in service of the sound.

S2D01 comes from “Studio1”.

The name refers to Studio One, the historic Jamaican studio and label connected to Clement “Coxsone” Dodd.

For Simone, the reference is also personal: the studio as a living place where sound identity, recording and memory meet.

Music, sound, software.

Alongside music production and sound design, S2D01 also develops MOODS.

MOODS is generative sound design software for macOS, created to generate continuous, layered and non-repeating sound environments from selected audio materials.
