About S2D01

S2D01 is the artistic practice of Simone Cappelli.

Based in Florence, Simone works across music, sound design and audio technology, creating ambient and experimental compositions, immersive sound environments and evolving listening systems.

Bio

Simone Cappelli

Simone Cappelli is an Italian musician, sound designer and audio technologist with more than four decades of experience at the intersection of music, technology and experimentation.

His path moves through analog instruments, radio broadcasting, electronic composition, digital audio, spatial sound and generative systems. Across these different phases, the focus has remained the same: sound as a space for atmosphere, memory and emotional perception.

Under the name S2D01, he develops ambient and experimental music, immersive sound design and evolving listening systems. His work combines handcrafted sonic materials, curated archives and contemporary audio processes to create environments that unfold over time.

Today, Simone’s practice ranges from music production and sound design to generative audio software and AI-assisted workflows, always keeping human sensitivity, curation and listening at the center.

Name origin

S2D01 stands for “Studio1”.

The name is a tribute to Clement “Coxsone” Dodd and the legendary Studio One label, one of the most influential recording studios in Jamaican music history.

For Simone, the reference is not only historical. It points to the studio as a living creative space: a place where sound identity, recording culture and emotional memory come together.

Closing

A long-term field of research.

S2D01 is not limited to one genre or format.

It is a long-term field of research where music, sound design, archives and technology meet to create deeper forms of listening.
